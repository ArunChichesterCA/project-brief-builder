Project title: Room Booking Helper
Problem: Staff cannot easily see which fictional training rooms are available.
Users: Office staff in a fictional organisation.
Outcome: Staff can quickly identify a suitable available room.
Essential features:
Show a list of rooms
Filter by capacity
Clearly show availability
Optional features:
Filter by equipment
Out of scope:
No live calendar connection
No actual bookings
Constraints:
Use fictional room data only
No personal information
Acceptance criteria:
At least three fictional rooms appear
The capacity filter works
The app shows a helpful message when there are no matches
